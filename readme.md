# Polity Atlas

A dependency-free, single-page site that maps Indian political parties and
freedom-movement thinkers onto the country's core debates.

Loktantra Atlas maps India's political parties and freedom-era thinkers across the debates like the state vs the market, faith vs the secular or the Centre vs the states, that continue to shape the republic. But no party is a single idea. Each is a bundle of positions. You may favour one party's free-market economics yet reject its cultural stance, or admire another's social vision while doubting its regulated economy. That tension isn't a flaw in you; it's the whole point. This atlas helps you think issue by issue and draw your own map
