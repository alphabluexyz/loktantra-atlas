Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

Loktantra Atlas is an independent, non-partisan educational project that maps Indian political thought. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), we act as the "Data Fiduciary" for any personal data you share with us.

2. What data we collect

We keep data collection to a minimum:

  • Email address — only if you choose to subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Your message — if you write to us, we see the email you send from.
  • Donations — UPI payments are handled entirely by your UPI app/provider. We do not collect or store your bank, card, or payment details.

We do not use tracking cookies or analytics that identify you.

3. Why we collect it

Your email is used only to send you the newsletter you signed up for, along with occasional updates about the Atlas. We never sell, rent, or share your email with anyone for marketing.

4. How we store and protect it

Subscriber emails are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the site owner, and transmitted securely. We keep your data only for as long as you remain subscribed, and take reasonable safeguards against unauthorised access.

5. Third-party services

A few trusted services help the site run, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google Forms & Sheets — collects and stores newsletter sign-ups.
  • Google Translate — provides on-page translation.
  • Cloudflare — hosts and secures the website.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023 you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it if it is inaccurate.
  • Erase it — unsubscribe and we will delete your email.
  • Grievance redressal — raise any concern and we will respond.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at connect@loktantraatlas.com.

7. Unsubscribing

You can opt out at any time by replying to any newsletter email with the word "unsubscribe", or by emailing us directly. We will remove you promptly — no questions asked.

8. Children's privacy

The Atlas is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any meaningful change will be reflected here with a new "last updated" date.

10. Contact

Questions, concerns, or requests? Write to us at connect@loktantraatlas.com. We're a small project and we read every message.
